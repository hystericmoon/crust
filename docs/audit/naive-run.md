# Naive run: first-time designer, screenshots only

## Step 1. Choose a starting point
I expect a welcome screen asking "what kind of site?". There is none (S01-S35 not present: no screen to judge for first run).
What I see: S05 "Your sites" lists one site, "Harbour Studio", 1 page. Then S08 "Start from a template" (reached via the site menu, S85).
S08 is good: three picture cards (Corner, Harbour, Signal) with one-line descriptions. Clicking one opens S09, a real preview with Desktop/Tablet/Phone and a page list, plus a clear "Use this template" button. I like this; it is the best moment in the app.
Trouble: S08 also shows "sample-site: This template can't be used: The photo atrium.jpg is used but isn't in the template." That reads like a crash report on my first screen. And "Open a template file... A .crust file" is a dead end for me.
S12 shows a paid template ("Get this template - US$49, opens in your browser") next to free ones; I did not expect money here. I'd hesitate.
I would stop reading at the description text ("Young Serif, navy on cream", "Fraunces on warm stone"): that is font talk, not mine to decide.
Confusing: the header says "Crust demo" while S05 says my site is "Harbour Studio". Which site am I in?

## Step 2. Change the headline and a photo
I expect to click the big headline and type. S45 shows the editor with the page big in the middle, but I see no hint to click the headline. The left rail offers "This page" and "Your site"; "Your site" shows two thumbnails under "MEDIA" with the tip "To put a photo on the page, select an image and press Choose image." So photos are handled in a library, not by clicking the photo. I'd guess, click the photo on the page.
S71 "Quick edit" is the friendliest: a plain form with "Heading: Build fast sites, visually" and a Save button. I would find it only if I clicked the page row in Pages (no screen shows how I get there). I'd happily use it. But its Heading text ("Build fast sites, visually") differs from the headline in S45 ("We make brands people talk about"), so I'm unsure which is the real headline.
Photo: S43 "Choose an image" has Upload photos, thumbnails, then a tall side panel with a crop idea ("Press the part that must stay in view", Wide/Square/Tall, Centre), required "Alt text" box, then "Use this photo", and right under it a red "Delete photo / Keep it" prompt already asking me about deleting. That frightens me.
The image is tagged "Needs alt" in red, and I cannot proceed without writing a description. Fair idea, but nobody told me why before.
The crop section is cut off at the top, so I cannot see what I'm cropping.
Where I'd give up: if the page is not obviously clickable; I'd bounce around S45 looking for an "edit text" control.
"Kept in this browser" next to Preview (S45, S43): did my change save? S13 later says "Saving isn't switched on yet." Scary: I may lose my work.

## Step 3. Add a page
I expect "New page" top right. S13 has it (and S85 the same). Good, visible.
What happens after clicking: no screen to judge (S37 is "New post" with Title/Address, not New page). I'd assume it is similar.
S13 page list: Work, Services, Studio, Contact, Home, "Page not found". Weird: Home sits under "NOT IN THE MENU", and tags say "Built into the app". I don't know what that means; are these my pages or demo pages? "Page not found ... Shown for addresses with no page" is a page I never asked for.
Adding a section to a page (S77, S76) is a dialog "Add a section" with tabs Layouts / Mine, "Type: Linked", "After Linked section". It opens on an empty "No linked sections yet", so I see nothing to add. I'd close it.
S78: "There's no linked section called 'hero-band'" - a dead end with only Back.
Drag handles appear for menu order (S13); no hint of a non-drag way, no screen to judge.

## Step 4. Put the site online
I expect one button: Publish. It exists everywhere (top right, S13, S45). Good.
S13/S59: clicking leads to a card saying "Publishing from here switches on together with saving, after the one-time setup in docs/setup-saving.md. Until then the live site doesn't change." I would stop right here: I don't know what docs/setup-saving.md is, and I cannot "do setup". Give-up point #1.
S67 (other site) is better: three choices, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Your own web host, plus "Export site folder" and "Preview in browser". But "Connect..." gives me no hint which is easiest or free; the intro says "a free host" without saying which.
S61: Cloudflare asks me to open another website, create a "Custom Token", set "Permissions: Account, Cloudflare Pages, Edit", copy and paste. Give-up point #2 (I'd have to make an account first, and nothing tells me that).
S63: own web host asks for SFTP/FTPS/FTP, Port, Server, "Folder on the server (public_html)". I'd close it.
Reassuring: "Saved, not live yet" status on the page row (S67) is clear and honest.
"Your site's address isn't set yet... example.pages.dev. Set it in Site settings -> Site address" - I don't know what address I should type.
Nothing says "your site is live at this link" because I cannot reach that moment: no screen to judge for success.
