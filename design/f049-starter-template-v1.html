        Recommendation

        A · Practice. It fits the widest range of small studios and service businesses, so it's the best first template to sell and to show what Crust can do. Its square buttons and outlined cards are restrained enough that buyers can make it theirs with a preset or a colour change. B is the safer pick for professional services. C has the most personality but suits a narrower audience; it makes a good second template.
