The first starter template

Three directions for the template that ships with Crust: Home, About, Services, Work, Contact, a blog with one post and a linked contact strip. Every colour, font and shape below is a value Crust's Site settings can hold, so what you see is what the template would publish.

Directions

A · Harbour: calm and editorial

Old-style serif headings, a humanist sans for text, deep green on warm paper. Square buttons, outlined cards, thick link underlines. Lots of air.

Suits studios, consultants, architects, therapists: anyone selling judgement. Easiest to make your own because nothing in it shouts.

B · Signal: bold and current

Heavy geometric sans set tight, a dark opening section, one burnt-orange accent. Pill buttons, soft-shadow cards, dramatic type scale.

Suits agencies, product studios, events. Strong first impression; harder to adapt to quieter businesses, and dark-first pages need good photos.

C · Corner: warm and local

Sturdy transitional serif, navy on butter yellow, highlighter-style links, rounded corners, flat filled cards.

Suits shops, cafés, makers, local services. Friendly and very readable; the yellow sets a mood some businesses won't want.

All pass WCAG AA (4.5:1). B's orange is the closest to the line, so it's the one to avoid using for small text on the grey surface. Fonts are system font stacks: they look like this on a Mac and fall back to close relatives elsewhere, with no web-font download (good for the 95+ speed score).

The same in every direction

Pages

Home, About (story, team of three), Services (three services with prices “from …”), Work (six projects), Contact (form, address, hours), a Journal with one post.

Built for replacing

Every sample word and photo is marked “Replace me”. Text is written as instructions (“A short line on this service…”) rather than fake copy, so nothing fake survives by accident.

Checked

It must pass the template quality check (F047): axe at three widths, contrast in every section style, one h1 per page, alt text, Lighthouse 95+ on Home.

Why I'd pick A

A first starter template should fit the widest range of businesses and be easy to make your own. Harbour's restraint does both: swap the green for a brand colour and it still works, which is less true of Signal's dark, loud opening or Corner's yellow. It also has the most contrast headroom. If Crust later ships several templates, B and C are good second and third ones.

Tell me A, B or C (or a mix, like “A with C's rounded buttons”), and I'll build it.
