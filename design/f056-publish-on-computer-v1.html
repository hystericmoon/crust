F056 · Milestone 6 · design snapshot

Publish on your computer

In the app, the Publish card on the Pages screen turns your saved pages into a finished website without GitHub: Preview in browser opens it in Safari or Chrome, Export site folder writes it to a folder you choose. Publishing straight to a host comes with F058 and F059. Three ways to lay out the card; A is recommended and being built.

Why A

Consistency

Same card, same spot, same heading as on crust-admin.pages.dev (Nielsen #4, Jakob's law). When F058 adds "Publish to Cloudflare Pages", it becomes the primary button and these two step back.

Recognition over recall

Both actions are visible, with their consequence in the label: one opens your browser, one writes files. No menu to remember (C) and no extra "Check" step (B).

Preview never blocks

Preview shows the site even with problems, and lists them. Only Export, the "finished" step, stops on them, like publishing does. Explore freely, fix before it counts.

Accessibility

  • Status changes are announced (role="status"); a stopped export is an alert and lists each problem with its page.
  • "Preview in browser" says it opens another app, for screen readers too. Buttons are 44 px tall (WCAG 2.5.8).
  • The Replace dialog takes focus on Replace, closes with Escape, and returns focus to Export site folder (2.4.3).
  • The spinner stops with reduced motion; the words carry the state, not the spinner.
