Why A

The templates are the product, so the store should frame them and stay out of their way. That's why shops for themes and photos use plain white (ThemeForest, Unsplash, Apple's app pages). Neutral surroundings make the item's own colours easier to judge, and that's what people buy on.

Built with Crust

A uses the Style library look Studio · Paper (white, near-black, Geist) with display headings and mono labels. Every page is ordinary Crust blocks, so the store also shows what Crust can do.

Accessibility

Black on white is 18:1. Every template picture needs a description, or publishing stops. Prices are text, not pictures, so they're readable without JavaScript. axe and Lighthouse run on every store page.
