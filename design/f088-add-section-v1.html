How it works

  • The Add icon opens pictures of whole sections, in the same left panel. No dialog.
  • Click a picture and it lands after the selected section (or at the end).
  • The "+ Add section" bars between sections choose the spot first, then open this panel.
  • "Add an element" sits under the pictures for single headings, text or images.

Why

  • Recognition over recall (Nielsen): people know "the one with three columns" when they see it, not by a block name.
  • The page stays visible while you choose, so you see where it goes. A modal hides it.
  • Reuses the existing gallery, search and "My layouts", so less to learn and less to break.

Keyboard and screen readers

  • Every picture is a real button with a name and description; Enter inserts, then focus moves to the new section.
  • A live message says "Added Three features after Hero."
  • "+ Add section" bars are reachable with Tab, not only the mouse.
  • Targets at least 24 px (WCAG 2.2 target size).

Trade-off

  • Fastest for one add, right where your eye is.
  • Only two or three pictures fit, so browsing and search are cramped, and a popover is harder for keyboard and screen reader users to get right.

Recommendation: A

The page stays in view, the pictures are always one click away in the Add panel, and it reuses the gallery you already have. I will keep the full-size gallery as a "See larger" link from the panel for people who want big previews.
