F060 · Milestone 6 · design snapshot

First run

The very first time someone opens the Crust app, there's no site yet. In under a minute they should pick a starting point, name the site and be in the builder, looking at the sample words and photos to swap for their own.

No account, no sign-up, nothing to connect. It shows once: as soon as the first site exists, the app opens straight into it, as it does today.

Why A

Fewest steps to a real site

Two inputs, one button. The milestone promise is "see it in the builder within a minute"; A gets there in three actions (pick, type, Enter), B in five.

Recognition over recall

Everything is visible at once (Nielsen's heuristic 6). In a wizard you can't see the name step while picking, or the templates while naming.

A good empty state does the work

C is a correct empty state, but first-run research (e.g. NN/g on onboarding) favours showing the payoff first: the templates are the welcome.

Keyboard and screen readers

WhatHow
Picking a startA real radio group: Tab into it, arrow keys move between Harbour, Signal, Corner and Blank. Each option reads its name and what's in it ("Harbour, studio, 5 pages, 9 photos").
NameA labelled field; problems (empty, too long) are said under it and linked with aria-describedby. Enter makes the site.
ProgressA live status line ("Adding photos (6 of 9)"); the button is disabled while it works.
LandingFocus moves to the "To replace" heading, so a screen reader announces where you are.
Contrast and targetsSelected card: 2px accent border plus a filled radio dot (not colour alone). Every card and button at least 44 px tall.
