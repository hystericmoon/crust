F066 Checkout: two decisions

Buy buttons on the store open the payment company's own checkout page. They sell on your behalf, add sales tax or VAT for the buyer's country, send the receipt, and pay you out. Nothing runs on our side except a link, so the store stays a plain static site and works without JavaScript.

1. Which payment company

All three are "merchants of record" (they handle tax worldwide), charge per sale with no monthly fee, and have a test mode. What separates them for you is payouts to Indonesia and whether a plain link is enough.

2. How the store shows test mode

Until you say "take real payments", the store's buttons go to the test checkout. Someone who finds the store meanwhile shouldn't think they've bought something. Drawn on a template page's price panel.
