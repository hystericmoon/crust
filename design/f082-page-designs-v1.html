Design the post page, the blog page and “Page not found”

Today these three have one fixed look: a centred title over the post, a plain list at /blog/, and a short “Page not found” message. With F082 you design them in the builder like any page. Three decisions: how designing works, which looks you start from, and where you find them.

Decision 2 · What you start from

Ready-made designs in the layout gallery

Until you open a design, your site keeps today's look (the first look in each row). When you do, these are offered as whole-page layouts, in your site's fonts and colours. Recommendation: offer all nine, start from today's look.

What I'll build: A, A, A

  • Designs with live blocks: Post title, Post details, Cover photo, Summary, Post content, Blog title, Blog intro and Post list (List, Cards, Featured; 2 or 3 across), plus every normal block.
  • Nine starting designs in the layout gallery; sites keep today's look until a design is saved.
  • Buttons on the Posts screen for the post and blog pages, and “Page not found” in the Pages list.
  • The builder shows your newest post (or a sample one) in the post design, and your real posts in the list.
  • Templates carry the designs. Say “B for decision 1” (or 2, 3) if you'd rather have another option.
