Recommended

A · Set the look once, pick per block

  • Site settings decides how Display headings, labels, buttons and cards look everywhere
  • Each block only picks which one: "Display" or "Normal", a label or none, which button style
  • Templates and the Style library (F072) set these values too

Why: one change restyles the whole site, so pages stay consistent. A visitor reads consistency as quality, and inconsistent sizes and weights are the most common sign of a home-made site. YOOtheme, Webflow classes and Figma text styles all use this model: shared styles plus a pick per element. Each block has two or three new choices, not ten.

What A adds, in detail

Headings
Weights 300 and 400 join 500–800. Tracking adds Tighter (−0.04 em) and Tightest (−0.05 em). A Heading can be Display size, which is a step above H1 that scales with the screen. Display has its own weight and tracking setting.
Labels
Optional "Small label above" on Headings, styled site-wide as Plain, Caps, Mono (Geist Mono, added only when used) or Chip. It's published inside the heading group, so screen readers read it with the heading.
Buttons
Primary and Outline as today, plus Soft (a tinted fill) and Text with arrow. The arrow is decorative and hidden from screen readers.
Cards
Card styles add Tile (a filled panel). A card can be "Photo fills the card", with its words on a dark scrim. The site-wide Card shadow is None, Soft or Lifted.
Old sites
Look exactly the same until you change something. The new settings are optional and default to today's look.
Accessibility
Labels are never below 12 px and keep 4.5:1 contrast, checked against your colours. Thin weights are for Display and H1–H2 only. Body text never goes below 400. Words on photo cards get a scrim, and the contrast check counts it.
