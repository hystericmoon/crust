The app in dark and light

You asked for React Bits' look with light and dark mode. Below are three takes, each drawn on the new editor (direction A) in both modes. Only the app changes: the cream site on the canvas keeps its own design in both modes. The app follows your computer's setting, and a sun/moon button switches it.

React Bits' code has a licence we can't use ("Commons Clause": no redistributing it, and our repository is public). So we copy the feel, not the code: Geist font (free licence), violet-tinted near-black, see-through panels, thin borders, 10–12px corners, monospace small print.

Contrast, worked out from the colours above

Every text pair, both modes

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for outlines and icons. See-through colours are mixed over their panel before measuring. Gradients are measured at their lighter end.

What gets built in F085

Take A, everywhere in the app

  • All app colours become named tokens, with a dark set and a light set; the four stray accents (purple, indigo, teal, orange) go.
  • Follows the computer's light/dark setting; the sun/moon button overrides it, and the app remembers your choice. No flash of the wrong mode on start.
  • Geist and Geist Mono bundled with the app (works offline in the desktop app).
  • The canvas keeps your site's own colours; only the frame around it changes.
  • A test checks every text pair at 4.5:1 in both modes, and the builder accessibility check runs in both.
  • Today's screens get the new look straight away. The new layout (top bar, icon bar) comes in F086–F087.
