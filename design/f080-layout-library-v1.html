Choice 1 · Photos in layouts

About half of the new sections are made around a photo. What should you get when you insert one?

RecommendedA · Sample photos, marked “to replace”

StudioRooms that hold the lightInteriors for small homes.See the work
To replace
  • The section looks finished the moment it lands, so you judge the layout, not a grey box.
  • The photos (public domain, from our templates) are copied into your Media library; the builder lists them under “To replace”.
  • Publishing waits until each is replaced or you press “Keep as is”: no stock photo goes live by accident.

Accessibility

Headings stay in order

A layout's h1 becomes h2 when the page already has one (as now). Each section starts at h2 and goes down from there; whole pages are checked for one h1 and no skipped levels.

Text on photos

Every photo section has a scrim, and its contrast is checked against the worst pixel, as Sections v2 does. Sample photos carry real descriptions; replacing a photo asks for yours.

Keyboard

The gallery keeps its Layouts button, arrow keys between tiles and the “Adds between …” line. Saved styles are a normal menu button with a list.

Speed

A layout with a slider or a map adds its small script only to pages that use it (as F079). Sample photos are published in the same resized copies as your own.
