Choosing fonts in Crust: three directions

Crust will ship 32 open-licence typefaces in tested pairs, served from the site itself. No Google requests and nothing for visitors to accept. The open question is how you pick from them in Site settings → Typography.

Recommended

A · Pairs first, fine-tune after

  • Twelve tested pairs as cards, each set in its own fonts
  • Heading and body dropdowns below, grouped Serif · Sans · Mono · On the visitor's device, each name shown in its font
  • The size it adds, in plain words

Why: 32 fonts make about 1,000 combinations, and most pairs don't work. Pair cards turn recall into recognition (Nielsen's heuristic 6) and cut the choice to twelve good options (choice overload, Iyengar and Lepper). You still have full control one step down. This is also how Squarespace and Framer present type.

How it works, whichever you pick

Fonts
Serif: Fraunces, Newsreader, Playfair Display, EB Garamond, Cormorant Garamond, Source Serif 4, Literata, Lora, Bodoni Moda, Instrument Serif, DM Serif Display, Young Serif. Sans: Geist, Inter Tight, DM Sans, Instrument Sans, Schibsted Grotesk, Manrope, Figtree, Onest, Hanken Grotesk, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk, Sora, Outfit, Public Sans, Work Sans, Bricolage Grotesque. Mono: Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, DM Mono.
Speed
Only the fonts a site uses are published: Latin and Latin-extended, all weights in one file each. The main ones load first, and text shows straight away in a fallback font. Lighthouse must stay at 95 or more.
Privacy
The files come from your own site, never from Google, so there's nothing to consent to.
Old sites
Sites keep their system fonts until you pick a pair. Templates and theme packs carry the choice by name.
Accessibility
Text keeps its sizes. Very thin weights are offered for display headings only, never for body text.
Licence
SIL Open Font License 1.1, approved by you on 2 Nov. Each font's licence ships with Crust.
