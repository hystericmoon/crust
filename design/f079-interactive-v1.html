Slideshow, Card slider, Tabs, Lightbox and Map

The first blocks that move or open. Each works as plain HTML first (photos you can swipe, all tab texts on the page, links to the photo and the map), and a small script, added only to pages that use the block, turns it into the interactive version. Four choices need a direction; the recommended one is outlined.

Accessibility built in

Slideshow and Card slider

Named regions (“Slideshow: Our rooms”), each slide labelled “2 of 5”, real buttons with names (“Next slide”). Playing on its own is off by default; when on, a pause button is always there and it stops while the pointer or keyboard is inside (WCAG 2.2.2). Reduce motion: no sliding, no autoplay.

Tabs

The WAI tabs pattern: arrow keys move between tabs, Tab goes into the text, Home/End jump. Without the script, every text is on the page under its own heading, so nothing is ever hidden from search engines or people without JavaScript.

Lightbox

A real dialog: focus moves in and comes back to the photo you pressed, Esc closes, arrow keys go through photos, the photo keeps its description. Without the script, pressing a photo opens the photo itself.

Map

The address is always written out (a map alone tells a screen reader nothing). The “Show map” button names the place; the map frame gets a title. Directions works without the map.
