F057 · Milestone 6 · design snapshot

Several sites

In the app you can keep one site per client and move between them. Each site is its own folder in Documents/Crust, with its own pages, history and publish settings. You can make a new site, rename, duplicate or delete one. A deleted site waits 30 days in Recently deleted, so it can be brought back.

Only in the desktop app. The web admin stays one site.

Why A

Frequency sets the cost

You switch sites a few times a day at most; you edit pages hundreds of times. The switch can take one extra click (A) instead of taking space from every screen (C) or every header (B).

One place for the whole job

Open, new, rename, duplicate, delete and restore all sit together, in the same table, buttons and "Recently deleted" pattern as the Pages screen (Nielsen #4, consistency). Nothing to learn twice.

Ready for first run

F060's welcome (pick a starter, name your site) ends on this screen's "New site" step. Later, the template gallery and hosts (F058) can also start here. B's menu would grow into a screen anyway.

Accessibility

  • The site list is a real table with a caption; each button names its site for screen readers ("Delete Bakery Lumière").
  • Dialogs take focus on the name field (or the safe button, Cancel, for Delete), close with Escape and give focus back to the button that opened them (WCAG 2.4.3).
  • Results ("Made Harbour Studio copy", "Restored") are announced through a status line; errors as alerts.
  • "All sites" is a link at the start of every Pages screen, first in tab order. Buttons are at least 36 px tall, with 8 px gaps (2.5.8).
  • The open site is marked in words ("Open"), not by colour alone (1.4.1).
