F068 Licence, terms and refunds: three decisions

Before anything has a price, the store needs to say what a buyer may do with a template, what happens if they change their mind, and who they're buying from. I've written plain-language drafts. You decide the wording and prices, and whether a lawyer reads them first. This isn't legal advice. Mockups use a made-up priced template called "Olive".

1. What one purchase allows

Nothing technical can stop a template being reused: Crust sites are plain files. So the licence is an honour system, and the real question is how much you ask honest buyers to count.

2. Refunds

Two facts shape this. Polar sells on your behalf and may refund a buyer within 60 days on its own to head off a card dispute, whatever your policy says. Polar also keeps its fee on a refunded order: about US$2.95 on a US$49 sale. Since F067, a full refund switches the buyer's download page off. A file they already downloaded stays on their computer.

This is the text built into the store pages. Highlighted bits are yours to fill in. Until you approve the wording, each page says "Draft" at the top, and the store refuses to take real payments.

Licence

When you buy a template from this store, you get a licence to use it. In short: make as many sites with it as you like, and don't pass the template itself on.

You can

Use the template for any number of websites, for yourself or for clients, paid or unpaid. Change anything in it. Keep the sites up for as long as you like, including after the licence ends for any reason.

You can't

Sell, share or give away the template file, or a copy of it with small changes. Offer it, or something made mainly from it, as a template of your own, in this store or anywhere else.

Fonts and photos

Fonts and sample photos come with their own free licences, which allow use on your sites.

Free templates

The templates that come with Crust are free. Use them for as many sites as you like.

Terms

Who you're buying from

Templates are made by your name or business. Payments are handled by Polar, which sells them on our behalf as the merchant of record. Polar adds tax where it applies and sends your receipt; Polar's buyer terms cover the payment.

What you get

A template file to open in Crust, the free app, and a personal download page. Updates to the template are free and appear on the same page.

If something is wrong

If a template doesn't open or doesn't match its store page, tell us and we'll fix it or refund you. Beyond that, templates come as they are, and we're not liable for more than you paid.

Changes and contact

If these terms change, the new version applies to purchases after the change. Questions: contact email.

Refunds

Not right for you? You can have a full refund within 14 days of buying, for any reason.

How

Reply to your receipt email, or write to contact email with your order number. The refund goes back to how you paid, usually within 5 to 10 working days.

What happens next

Your download page stops working, and the licence ends for any new sites. Sites you've already published can stay up.

After 14 days

If the template doesn't open or doesn't match its store page, we'll still fix it or refund you.
