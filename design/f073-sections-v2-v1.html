Milestone 8 · F073 Sections v2 · design sketch

Sections that reach the edge, move, and overlap

Six new things a Section can do: width, gradient backgrounds, a muted background video, a focal point on its photo, pulling up over the section above, and letting the header sit on top of the first section. Below: what each looks like on the published page, then three ways to put them in the Section's settings.

On the published page

Where each option lands

1 · Width

Narrow · text
Normal · today
Wide
Full · edge to edge

Narrow suits long reading (about 46 rem, near 70 characters). Wide is a quarter wider than the page. Full uses the whole screen with a small gutter. To run a photo right to the edge, use Left or Right edge.

2 · Reach one edge

Quiet rooms

The text stays on the page's grid (dashed lines). The photo column runs on to the right or left edge of the screen. On phones everything stacks.

3 · Gradient

Since 1998

From the section's style colour to another site colour: downward, diagonal, across, or a soft glow. Only colours that keep the text readable at both ends are offered.

4 · Background video

muted · loopsThe workshop❚❚

Silent and looping, with a pause button (WCAG 2.2.2). Reduce motion on, or no JavaScript: the still photo shows instead and the video never loads.

5 · Focal point

desktop
phone

You mark what matters in the photo. When a phone crops a wide photo, it keeps that spot in view instead of the middle.

6 · Pull up, header on top

AtelierWorkStudio
Rooms for living

The header sits on the first section's photo, in that section's text colour. The section below pulls up so its cards overlap the photo.

Header sits on this section

Only on the first section. Header text uses this section's colours.

The settings follow the order you think in: how wide, what's behind it, how it meets its neighbours. The Background kinds are a segmented control, so only one set of fields shows at a time. A video section asks for a still photo first, so Reduce motion always has something to show.

  • Progressive disclosure: the Style tab stays short. Gradient, photo and video fields appear only when you choose that kind.
  • Recognition over recall: widths are little pictures, not names you have to remember.
  • Error prevention: gradient colours that would fail contrast aren't offered. "Header sits on this section" appears only on a page's first section.
  • The canvas quick bar keeps Height and Image as they are, and its Image pop-up gains the focal point.

Details

How it works

Saved as
New optional Section props: width, gradient { to, direction }, videoSrc, focus { x, y }, overlap, headerOver. Sections without them publish exactly as before, so no migration is needed.
Video
Linked from a web address (https://…mp4 or .webm). The media library holds photos only for now; adding video uploads would make the Git repo heavy (proposal). The still photo is required. About 20 lines of JavaScript, only on pages with a video, start it when motion is allowed and add the pause button.
Header on top
Works on the page's first section only. The header loses its own background and takes the section's text colour, and the section gets extra top space so nothing hides under it. On that page the header scrolls away instead of sticking. A sticky header that changes colour as you scroll comes with F076.
Pull up
The section moves up by a spacing step (A little = M, Half = XL, A lot = 2×XL). The section above gets the same extra space at the bottom so its words aren't covered. Whatever overlaps needs its own background (a Card, or a Muted column), or its words would sit on the photo above.
Accessibility
Contrast is checked at both ends of a gradient and for the header over the photo (through the tint). The focal point has slider fields for the keyboard. The video has a real pause button with a label and never plays under Reduce motion. Width pictures have text labels.
