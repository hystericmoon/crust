"Save draft" goes away. Ctrl/⌘+S still saves straight away for people who are used to it. Site settings keep their Save and Discard buttons until F092 regroups that screen, because Discard there undoes several panels at once.

Accessibility

  • The top bar is a banner; the icon bar is a labelled toolbar; every icon button has a name ("Undo", "Phone width").
  • The device toggle is three toggle buttons with aria-pressed; the page switcher is a real select, so it works with the keyboard and screen readers as-is.
  • The site menu opens with Enter or Space, closes with Escape, and gives focus back to its button.
  • The status line is a polite live region: "Saving…" and "Saved" are spoken, but not every keystroke.
  • All targets are at least 28×28px (above the 24px minimum, WCAG 2.5.8); colours come from the F085 tokens, which already pass 4.5:1 in both modes.
