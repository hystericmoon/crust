Save a section you designed, or a whole page, and reuse it from the layout library. Pick where saving happens and where your layouts show up. Click a section on the page below to try it.

  • Copies, not linksEditing or deleting a saved layout never changes pages that already use it.

  • Saved to GitHubOne file per layout in content/layouts/. Without GitHub, kept in this browser.
  • Delete asks firstRename and Delete on each card; delete says what it does and doesn't touch.

A · On the section, own tab

  • Save right where you designed it
  • Your layouts are one click away, never buried among 20 built-ins
  • One more button on the section toolbar

B · All in the gallery

  • Nothing new on the canvas
  • Saving and reusing in one place
  • You have to know to open the gallery to save
  • Must select the section first, then go elsewhere

C · On the section, mixed in

  • A saved hero shows up under Hero, next to similar ones
  • Category to pick when saving (or a guess)
  • Harder to see everything you saved at once
Why A

Act where your attention is. You decide a section is worth keeping while you're looking at it, so the save belongs on that section (proximity, fewer steps). B splits one intent across two places.

Your own work is a different kind of thing. People look for "the one I made" by memory, not by category. A separate tab gives them a short list to recognise. YOOtheme, Webflow and Framer all keep saved items apart from the built-ins for this reason.

Keyboard: the save button is on the toolbar the keyboard already reaches (Enter on a section). The name field gets focus, and Enter saves.
