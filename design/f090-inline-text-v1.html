Leaving and the keyboard
  • Escape or Done or a click elsewhere leaves.
  • Keyboard: select the block, Enter to type, Escape to return to the block.
  • A "Type on the page" button sits in the block's bar for screen readers and touch.
  • Undo steps are shared with the side panel.
Works
  • What you type is what the page shows: same font, size, colour.
  • The bar is small and only for that block, no panel to look away to.
  • The side panel and Quick edit stay for everything else.
Costs
  • A bar above text near the top of the page flips below it.
  • Clicking text now opens typing. Moving a block is still by its handle or Alt and arrows.
