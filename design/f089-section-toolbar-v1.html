Works
  • Words, not icons: the owner is not a developer, labels are recognition over recall.
  • Five items fits in one row at 900px and wraps nicely on narrow canvases.
  • Site-wide actions are out of the way and always confirmed with a count.
Costs
  • One extra click to reach Height, Image or colour.
  • Row is about 440px wide, so on a phone canvas it sits above the section, not on it.

Recommendation: A. Same one row at every width, labelled, keyboard order Section → Style → Layout → Move → More (arrow keys inside the toolbar, Escape closes a menu). Colours show their names, swatches 24px inside 32px targets. Site-wide actions live under More and always show how many sections and pages they touch before anything changes.
