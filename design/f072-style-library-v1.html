Why: each card is a small piece of a real page in that look's own fonts and colours, so you compare looks by seeing them, not by reading names (recognition over recall). Twelve fit on one screen, which avoids choice overload better than a long list. The dots switch colourway in place. The live preview beside Site settings shows your own Home page, and Undo brings back your previous look. This is how YOOtheme's Style Library and Squarespace's site styles work.

How it works

Applying
Sets colours, font pair, type and detail options, button shape and look, cards, links and corner radius. Spacing, page width, your pages and your header menu stay as they are. Undo brings back the previous look.
After
Change anything afterwards, such as one colour or a font. The card then shows "Changed from Atelier".
The old presets
The 9 colour presets leave the screen. Sites using one keep their look.
Accessibility
Every colourway passes all seven contrast pairs and the section style checks. The cards are buttons with their name as the label, and the colourway dots are a labelled radio group, so they work by keyboard and screen reader.
