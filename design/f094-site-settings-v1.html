Site settings: edit search defaults and where to publish

F094. Today the sheet only shows these two groups and links elsewhere. Three ways to make them editable in place, drawn with the app's real colours. Only the two groups differ; the address, theme pack and template groups stay as they are.

Recommendation: C, summary rows that open in place

Every group shows its current value at a glance (what you'd check before publishing), and only the one you open shows fields. It matches the Fine-tune rows in the Styles panel, keeps the sheet short on a phone, and keeps the publishing password form out of sight until you ask for it. A is the simplest to build but the sheet gets long; B hides settings behind tabs you have to find.

Accessibility, whichever you pick

  • Every field has a visible label tied to it, errors say what to fix in words next to the field and are announced, and "Needs attention" is a word, not only a colour.
  • In C, each row's button says what it opens ("Edit Search defaults") and reports expanded or collapsed. Opening moves focus to the first field and Done returns it to the row's button.
  • Every control is at least 24px high (WCAG 2.2 target size) and the sheet stays a real dialog: Escape closes it, focus is trapped and returns to the Site settings button.
  • The description counter is text ("46 of about 155 characters"), not a colour change, and the picture needs alt text before Save works.

Decision for you: C, A or B? And are you happy to add the two optional site-file fields (description, sharing picture)? Without them, Search defaults would only edit the site name and language.
