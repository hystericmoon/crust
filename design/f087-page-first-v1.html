F087 · Page first

What you see when you open a site, and where a page's title and search settings live. All three takes open on the Home page in the editor, at full width (no 57% zoom), with blocks inside the Add panel. They differ in how "Next steps" shows up and where page settings open. I'll build take A unless you say otherwise.

A · One panel on the right that follows what you pick Recommended · building this

1 · New site opens, nothing picked
2 · Page settings (from the page switcher)
3 · Nothing picked, Next steps skipped
4 · A section picked
WhyOne place for "settings of the thing I'm on": a block, the page, or the to-do list. Figma, Framer and Webflow all work this way, so the rule is learnable in one go (consistency, Nielsen #4). The page gets the full width until you need a panel (focus on content, progressive disclosure).
How you get therePage settings: "Page settings" in the page switcher menu (and in the Pages panel later, F091). Esc or a click on the grey area around the page drops the selection and closes a block's panel. Next steps comes back from Help. Closing the panel (× or the panel button) never loses anything.
Trade-offThe canvas width changes when the panel opens. I keep the page at "fit width" so it rescales instead of jumping, and the panel slides in under 200 ms (off with Reduce motion).

B · Floating card and a page settings dialog Option

1 · New site opens
2 · Page settings
WhySquarespace's model: page settings are a separate, "formal" place, so they feel safe to change.
Trade-offThe card covers part of the page you're editing. The dialog hides the page, so you can't see the title change on the canvas, and it brings back Save/Cancel when everything else saves on its own.

C · Checklist strip on top, settings in the Pages panel Option

1 · New site opens
2 · Page settings
WhyWix's model: pages and their settings live together. The strip is always visible without taking width.
Trade-offPage settings depend on the Pages panel (F091) and hide two clicks deep. The strip costs height on every screen until it's done or skipped.

Accessibility in all takes

KeyboardEsc on a picked block goes back to "nothing picked"; the panel's first field gets focus when it opens from a menu, and focus returns to the switcher when it closes.
Screen readersThe panel is a labelled region ("Settings: Hero section", "Settings: Home page", "Next steps"); its title change is announced politely.
Next stepsReal checkboxes that tick themselves; Skip is a button, 44 px target; it remembers per site.
