Choice 1 · How a YouTube or Vimeo video appears before it plays

RecommendedA · Your cover photo

Play video: Our bakeryYouTube
  • The page loads nothing from YouTube until the visitor presses play (no cookies, no tracking, speed score unaffected).
  • Then it plays from youtube-nocookie.com in place.
  • Without a cover photo: a calm panel in the section's colours with the title.
  • Without JavaScript the button is a link to the video.

B · YouTube's own thumbnail

Our bakery
  • No photo to choose.
  • Every visit asks YouTube's servers for the picture, which tells YouTube who visited (a privacy notice issue in the EU).
  • Their thumbnail rarely matches the site's look.

C · Your own video files only

studio-tour.mp4CC
  • No outside services at all.
  • Video files are large; the site host serves every play (Cloudflare Pages allows up to 25 MB per file).
  • Most people already have their videos on YouTube or Vimeo.

All three: your own .mp4 always works too, as the browser's own player with preload="none" and a captions file (.vtt). A, B and C only differ for YouTube and Vimeo links.

Accessibility built in

Stats

Each figure and its label are read together as one list item (“1,200+ sites published”). The figure is real text in the heading font, not a picture. No counting animation: moving numbers are unreadable for a moment and announce nothing useful.

Video

The play button says what plays (“Play video: Our bakery”). Captions: a .vtt file for your own video (the builder reminds you if there's none and the video has speech); YouTube and Vimeo show their captions switched on. Nothing plays on its own and nothing makes sound before the press (WCAG 1.4.2, 2.2.2).

Table

Real <table> with <caption>, <th scope> for the header row and column. The phone scroll area is focusable and named, so keyboard users can scroll it (WCAG 2.1.1). Numbers line up (tabular figures) and align right.

Pricing

Each plan is a list item with its name as a heading. The highlight carries a written badge (WCAG 1.4.1). Buttons say which plan (“Choose Studio”), so a list of links still makes sense (WCAG 2.4.4). Prices are text, e.g. “€49 per month”.
