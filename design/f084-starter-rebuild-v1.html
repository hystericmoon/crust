Harbour, Signal and Corner, rebuilt on the new design layer

The three starter templates get the Milestone 8 fonts, looks, sections and blocks, full-length pages and finished copy for a believable business each. Same photos you picked. Below: each new Home page top to bottom, then three choices with my recommendation outlined.

What I'll build (unless you say otherwise)
  • Looks: each template uses a Style library look that matches its character: Harbour = Atelier · Stone, Signal = Studio · Signal, Corner = Bistro · Cream.
  • Pages: menus fit each business (Corner gets Menu, Workshops, Visit us instead of Services, Work, Contact).
  • Copy: finished words for a made-up business, still marked "to replace", so nobody publishes fake awards or prices by accident.
  • Each Home has 7–8 kinds of section (the new check wants 5), and each template has its own post, blog and "Page not found" designs.

1 · The look of each template

The Style library (F072) already has a look made for each kind of business. Fonts, colours, labels and button shapes all come from it.

A · Use the matching Style library lookRecommended
Harbour
Atelier · Stone
Signal
Studio · Signal
Corner
Bistro · Cream

Why: Site settings then shows the buyer which look they have, and the two other colourways are one click away, already contrast-checked. That's recognition over recall: they see their options instead of rebuilding colours by hand. The three looks are close to each template's colours today (deep green on paper, one hot accent, navy on butter).

B · Keep today's colours, new fonts only
Harbour
Own colours
Signal
Own colours
Corner
Own colours

Keeps each template's palette unique. But Site settings shows "Custom look", so the buyer gets no colourways to try, and Signal stays a white site with one dark section rather than the dark agency look that read as premium in your references.

3 · How finished the words are

Today the templates say "One line on this service and who it's for". The new ones read like a real business. The question is what the buyer sees in the "To replace" list.

A · Finished copy, still marked to replaceRecommended
To replace · Home
Heading “Design for places people remember”Edit · Keep as is
Figures “62 homes, studios and…”Edit · Keep as is
Quote “They listened longer than…”Edit · Keep as is
Background photoEdit · Keep as is

Why: the page looks finished in the gallery and in the builder, and publishing still waits until each made-up figure, quote, price and photo is replaced or kept on purpose. The template can't put fake awards or a stranger's quote on someone's live site.

B · Finished copy, nothing marked
To replace · Home
Nothing to replace✓

How Webflow templates work. Fastest to publish, but "62 homes" and "Ingrid Solberg" go live unless the buyer spots every one. Not safe for a tool aimed at non-designers.

C · Instructions as copy (today)
Heading
“One line on this service and who it's for.”

Clear about what to write, but it's the filler you called out: the page never looks finished, so the template doesn't sell itself.

Also in the rebuild

Every page is full length

Home 9–10 sections. Inner pages 5–7: Harbour's Services has prices and questions, Signal's Work a slideshow and named project tiles, Corner's Menu three price tables.

Post, blog and 404 designs

Harbour: classic post with the contact strip after it, blog as a list. Signal: title on the cover photo, blog with a featured post. Corner: title beside the cover, blog as cards. Two finished posts each.

Header, top bar and motion

Harbour's header sits over its photo; Signal adds a top bar and a "Start a project" button; Corner puts the logo on top like a shop sign. Motion Subtle (Harbour, Corner) and Lively (Signal), off under Reduce motion.

Accessibility

Every look passes WCAG AA contrast in all its section styles (tested with F072). Text on photos sits under a tint; the moving logo row has a pause switch; the slider and tabs work by keyboard. The template check runs axe on every page at three widths, Lighthouse on Home, and the new F083 rules (no empty slots, matching crops, 5+ kinds of section).
